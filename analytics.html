What it is

Brief
Data you can trust.

Most teams run on broken analytics and don't know it — events that double-fire, attribution that doesn't reconcile across platforms, dashboards nobody trusts enough to act on.

We rebuild the data layer first: GA4 configured properly, server-side events where it matters, and attribution that holds up under scrutiny — so every other decision in the funnel rests on something solid.

Approach
Audit to single source of truth.

Step 01 Audit Full review of existing tracking — what's firing, what's missing, what's lying. Most audits surface six figures in misattributed spend.
Step 02 Instrument Server-side and client-side events rebuilt against a single source of truth, mapped to the actions that actually matter to the business.
Step 03 Reconcile Attribution models cross-checked against ad platforms and CRM data until the numbers in every dashboard agree.
Step 04 Report Dashboards built for Monday morning, not board decks — the few numbers that tell you what to do next.
