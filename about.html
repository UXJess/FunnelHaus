About
The studio in brief.

An independent design & growth studio based in Vancouver. We turn service and product businesses into high-performing digital machines — through systems, not services.

Why we exist

Why we exist
Systems, not symptoms.

Most businesses don't have a growth problem — they have a systems problem. Disconnected tools, unclear messaging, decisions made on instinct instead of insight. The friction is everywhere, and a new logo or campaign won't fix it.

FunnelHaus exists to close that gap. We connect strategy, creative, and performance as one integrated system where every move supports the next — and the funnel compounds quarter after quarter.

Posture
Independent. By design.

We're a small studio on purpose. A handful of clients per quarter — never more — so the work stays deep, the systems stay sharp, and your team has direct access to the people doing it.

We work the way the best in-house teams work: strategy, design, build, and growth happen together, not as a relay race. We bring AI tools where they make us faster, taste where machines fall short, and the discipline to ship every week, not every quarter.

Principles
Four ways we work.

Principle 01 Discover, then decide. Clarity before execution. Audit, interviews, and goals — the work starts with understanding the problem, not the deliverable.
Principle 02 Structured thinking. Every brief becomes a system. Scope, sequence, deliverables, and the metric that says it worked — written down before the work begins.
Principle 03 Collaborate, don't hand off. Strategy, design, build, and growth happen together. No throws over the wall, no scope-creep theater. One team, one cadence.
Principle 04 Decisions grounded in insight. Trends are loud. Data is quieter. We work from research, evidence, and what the funnel says — not what's hot this quarter.

Studio
Based in Canada. Working globally.

FunnelHaus is headquartered in Vancouver, BC, and serves clients across North America and beyond. We work asynchronously by default and on-site when the work calls for it — coast-to-coast and across time zones.

Engagements run on quarterly sprints. We take a small number of new clients each quarter, which means availability is real but limited. If timing matters, get in touch early — most of our work books a quarter ahead.
